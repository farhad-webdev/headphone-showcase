The project includes headphone.svg as a built-in product illustration, so the
showcase works immediately without external image files.

To use your own product photography, put transparent PNG or WebP images here
and update the ASSETS paths in src/App.jsx. Recommended filenames:

headphone-01.png
headphone-02.png
headphone-03.png
headphone-04.png

Recommended:
- PNG or WebP
- Transparent background
- High resolution
- Same product photographed from different angles
- Keep the headphone centered in each image

If you only have 3 images, you can reuse one of them in App.jsx.
